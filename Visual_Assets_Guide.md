### For Unsplash images:
1. Click the link → Click **"Download free"** (top right)
2. Select **Original** size
3. Rename to the suggested filename

### For Pexels images/videos:
1. Click the link → Click **"Free Download"** (green button)
2. Select **Original** resolution
3. Rename to the suggested filename

### For Pixabay videos:
1. Click the link → Browse the collection → Click your favorite
2. Click **"Download"** → Select **1920x1080** or higher
3. Rename to the suggested filename
