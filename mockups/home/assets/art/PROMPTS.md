# Aletheia image series — "The Uncovering"

Twelve still-life photographs made for the site itself. One set, one light, one palette.
They stand for how we think and build; they never pretend to be staff, clients or client work.

**How to use:** paste the STYLE block and one SUBJECT line per image into Codex (image generation),
ChatGPT or any image model. Save each result into this folder with the exact filename
(`.png` or `.jpg`, at least 1500 px on the long side). The page picks them up automatically;
until a file exists, its slot shows a colour tile with the shot number.

---

## STYLE (paste with every prompt, unchanged)

Editorial still-life photograph, medium-format camera, 80mm lens, f/8, everything in sharp focus.
Hard late-afternoon sunlight from the upper left casting crisp, long shadows. Seamless matte paper
backdrop and tabletop in one flat colour. Real, tactile materials with visible texture: thick cotton
paper, raw plaster, brass, solid cobalt-blue glass, cloth. Calm, restrained composition with generous
negative space; nothing cluttered. Natural colour, subtle film grain, true photographic realism.
Palette strictly limited to warm ivory (#F5F0E6), cobalt blue (#2747FF), marigold yellow (#FFB400),
vermilion red-orange (#F24B2A) and deep indigo (#0F1136). If hands appear they are real adult hands
with a natural South Asian skin tone, relaxed and unposed, no jewellery, no nail polish, anatomically
correct. Never show a face. No text, letters, numbers, logos, screens, phones, laptops, robots, glowing
effects, circuit patterns, lens flare, 3D-render look or clay look.

## Reel film (optional, 15–20 s, replaces the stand-in video)

Same set, same light, same palette; one continuous mood, cut as four shots of 4–5 s each.
Deliver landscape 16:9 (1920×1080) and, if possible, portrait 9:16 for phones. Mute.

1. Hands peel back the cobalt sheet to reveal marigold (a01, in motion, slow).
2. From above, the pencil finishes a wireframe line; a petal drifts in (a02).
3. A hand sets the brass sphere on the plaster steps; the shadow settles (a04).
4. The stamp comes down on the ledger and lifts away, leaving a vermilion mark (a09).

Video-model prompt: "Editorial still-life film, medium-format look, 80mm, f/8, hard late-afternoon
sunlight from the upper left, long crisp shadows, warm ivory set, cobalt / marigold / vermilion /
indigo objects only, real South Asian hands, slow deliberate movements, locked-off camera, no faces,
no text, no screens. Shot: …" — then the shot line.

If you'd rather shoot it on a phone: one window, afternoon, a sheet of ivory card as the table,
coloured card from a stationery shop, a brass bell/key from the house. Lock the phone on a stack of
books; move slowly. I'll grade it.
